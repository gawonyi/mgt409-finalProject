Raise your hand before the real class does.

A voice-first mock class: an AI professor styled like yours + AI classmates, in a pixel classroom.

Gawon Yi · MGT 409 Final Project · https://github.com/gawonyi/mgt409-finalProject

The problem

More of my classes became discussion-heavy.

  • As a non-native speaker, the tempo was unfamiliar
  • Hard to jump in at the right moment with the right content
  • By the time my point is phrased, the discussion has moved on

We can prepare the analysis alone. We can't practice the live, spoken part: getting the floor, follow-ups, building on a classmate in 30 seconds.

What's new

  • A room, not a chat: professor + 1–3 classmates, real hand-raise rule, cold calls, follow-ups
  • Your professor: describe the style + 5 sliders
  • Line-by-line coaching on how you said it

vs ChatGPT voice (1:1), Second Nature (one avatar, sold to schools), HBS/NUS simulations (faculty-built), pixel agent offices (decoration only)

How the agents work

  • Code decides who may speak (eligibility, cold call, raised-hand priority, caps)
  • Professor picks only from that list
  • Classmates: one batched hand-raise call, remarks cite pack facts
  • Pack Builder + Coach; gpt-6-luna via Portkey

Safety

  • Injection: materials & transcripts treated as data
  • No graded work: write-up requests refused in code
  • No made-up facts: remarks must cite pack IDs
  • No loops / no grabbing the floor: code-owned turn rules, cap 14 turns
  • Budget: worst-case check, $0.40 + $0.10 reserve, hard cap $0.50
